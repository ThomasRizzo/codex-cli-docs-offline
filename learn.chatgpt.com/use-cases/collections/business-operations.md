Use ChatGPT Work or Codex to bring together project trackers, KPI dashboards,
planning documents, meeting notes, and stakeholder context. ChatGPT can turn
that operating record into reviewable briefs, decisions, models, and updates
while keeping assumptions and open questions visible.
