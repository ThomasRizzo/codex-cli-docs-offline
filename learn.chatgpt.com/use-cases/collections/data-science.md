Turn questions, dashboards, and raw data into review-ready analysis.

Use ChatGPT Work or Codex to turn dashboards, metric definitions, exports,
experiment notes, and stakeholder context into analysis that others can review
and act on. ChatGPT can keep calculations, sources, caveats, and open questions
visible so analysts can validate the evidence before sharing it.
