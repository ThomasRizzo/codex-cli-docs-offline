# Sales

> For the complete documentation index, see [llms.txt](/llms.txt). Markdown versions of documentation pages are available by appending `.md` to the page URL.

Turn account context and deal signals into pipeline actions.

Use ChatGPT Work or Codex to bring together account records, customer
conversations, pipeline signals, and open risks. ChatGPT can turn approved
context into briefs, follow-ups, forecast reviews, and account plans while
keeping sourced facts separate from inferred opportunity or risk.

## Prioritize pipeline and forecast

Rank the accounts that need attention, then review which deals should remain in
commit, move to upside, or come out of the forecast.

- [Prioritize accounts](https://developers.openai.com/codex/use-cases/prioritize-accounts): Give ChatGPT account records, customer conversations, usage signals, renewal or growth...
- [Review forecast risk](https://developers.openai.com/codex/use-cases/forecast-risk-review): Give ChatGPT forecast snapshots, opportunity records, call notes, deal threads, email...

## Prepare and follow up on meetings

Gather the context for a customer meeting, then turn approved notes or a
transcript into follow-up drafts, an internal recap, and reviewable next steps.

- [Prepare meeting briefs](https://developers.openai.com/codex/use-cases/meeting-prep-briefs): Use ChatGPT with Calendar, Drive, Slack, and Gmail to gather approved sources before a...
- [Turn meetings into follow-ups](https://developers.openai.com/codex/use-cases/zoom-meeting-follow-ups): Use ChatGPT with Zoom transcripts and AI Companion summaries to draft customer follow-up...
