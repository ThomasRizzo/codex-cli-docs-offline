Build process

Build process

  1. Initial prompt

    Defined the project brief, scope, and intended experience.

    Generate a React app that's a cinematic experience flying over the Golden Gate Bridge. Make the 3D visualization highly realistic.

  2. Iteration 1

    Created a realistic cinematic Golden Gate Bridge flight scene with detailed bridge structure, bay water, terrain, lighting, haze, traffic, and camera motion.

  3. Iteration 2

    Added Auto Pilot and Manual flight modes, with keyboard, mouse, scroll, boost, altitude, and viewpoint controls.

  4. Iteration 3

    Improved manual navigation by fixing camera tilt, correcting lateral movement, and smoothing scroll-based motion.

  5. Iteration 4

    Polished the overlays with a liquid-glass controls panel, clearer telemetry, improved readability, and a cleaner title treatment.

  6. Iteration 5

    Refined the environment so land, water, terrain, and viewport coverage felt more natural and cinematic.

  7. Iteration 6

    Tuned the bridge visuals, haze, exposure, suspension cables, and anchorage details so the structure felt physically grounded.

  8. Final pass

    Finished the cinematic Golden Gate flight experience with realistic bridge geometry, grounded suspension details, and stable Auto Pilot and Manual flight modes.
